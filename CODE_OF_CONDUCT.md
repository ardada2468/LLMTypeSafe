# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, keep criticism about the work
rather than the person, and do not harass anyone. Maintainers may remove
comments, commits, issues and contributions that do not meet this standard, and
may ban repeat or serious offenders.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/ardada2468/LLMTypeSafe/security/advisories/new)
marked "Code of Conduct" — it is the repository's confidential channel. Reports
are reviewed promptly and the reporter's identity is kept confidential.
